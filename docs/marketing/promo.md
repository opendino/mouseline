## Long description

**Draw a line. Get it done.**

MouseLine brings mouse gestures to the Mac, done the way a Mac app should be. Hold the right
mouse button, draw a shape, release. A line to the left snaps the window to the left half of
the screen; up maximizes it; down minimizes it. Every shape can do something else: go back
in Safari, skip a song, lock the screen, press a shortcut, open an app or a URL, or run a
shell command or an AppleScript.

- **32 built-in shapes**, plus your own: record any stroke and give it an action.
- **Learns your hand.** A 30-second calibration with your real mouse button tunes the
  recognition to how precisely and how long you draw.
- **Different gestures per app.** Back in the browser, close tab in your editor — the app
  under the cursor decides.
- **Out of the way.** A quick click is still a click; hold still and the context menu opens
  as usual. Turn gestures off in games, 3D tools and remote desktops.
- **Feels native.** Liquid Glass on macOS 26, a System Settings-style window, a glowing trail
  while you draw, light and dark themes, 20 languages.
- **Private by design.** No network, no analytics. The keyboard is only observed while you
  draw, to catch Esc.
- **Free and open source** (Apache 2.0).

Requires macOS 13 or later; Apple silicon and Intel.
